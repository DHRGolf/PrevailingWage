Township corners come from BLM's public California PLSS map service (gis.blm.gov). State-line legs follow the surveyed Oregon and Nevada boundaries. Legs that run into the Pacific are closed offshore, then the whole boundary is clipped to California's coastline from the Census Bureau's state outline. All sources are federal, free, and need no login or key.

This tool is a screening aid. It does not replace a written classification from the DIR.
